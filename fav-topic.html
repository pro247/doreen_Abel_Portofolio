Why TinyML is the Future of Microcontrollers in Malawi

By Doreen Providence Abel — Year 5 Electronics & Computer Engineering

1. What Malawi is Right Now (The Cloud Bottleneck)

Traditional AI relies heavily on external cloud servers. In Malawi, this cloud-dependent model struggles constantly due to frequent power blackouts and unstable network coverage in rural areas. When internet connection drops or load shedding occurs, remote monitoring systems fail completely, making cloud-based infrastructure highly unreliable for critical monitoring operations.

2. The Future of Microcontrollers (Edge Intelligence)

TinyML fixes this problem by running optimized AI models directly on low-power microcontrollers like the ESP32. These intelligent edge devices analyze sensor data locally without needing any internet connection. This enables autonomous, low-cost engineering solutions built specifically to survive and thrive in Malawi's localized environment.
