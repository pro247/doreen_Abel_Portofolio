Why TinyML is the Future of Microcontrollers in Malawi

Traditional AI relies on external cloud servers, which struggle in Malawi due to frequent power blackouts and unstable rural networks. TinyML fixes this infrastructure flaw by running highly optimized neural network models directly on low-power microcontrollers like the ESP32, processing environmental data locally without requiring internet data packets.
